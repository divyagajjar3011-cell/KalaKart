Where Craft Meets

A marketplace made for makers.

KalaKart is an online marketplace concept created to connect local artisans and handmade creators directly with customers. Instead of letting beautiful handmade work stay hidden in small circles, KalaKart gives makers a digital space to present their products, stories and skills.

From resin art and crochet to handwork clothing, candles, glass décor and customized gifts, every product celebrates the time and creativity behind handmade work.
